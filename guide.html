MLcon week · 17–20 November 2026

One conference, three rock nights, and a Berlin worth a second chance.

A day-by-day plan built around the talks, the gigs, and the pubs in between, plus what's on in the city this week.

  • Base: Maritim proArte, Friedrichstraße
  • Land Tue ~12:00 · leave Fri morning
  • Dark by about 16:15 · bring a warm coat

The last time in Berlin didn't land. This time the plan skips the checklist sights and goes where the city is strongest after dark: loud rooms, good beer and neighbourhoods that only wake up when the conference ends.

Days belong to MLcon. Evenings start around six and go south and east: Kreuzberg and Neukölln for the gigs, Mitte for the jazz cellars a few minutes from the hotel. Every stop below links straight to Google Maps directions, and the same plans load in the map app under Plan.

Getting in from BER: the airport is in fare zone C, so buy an ABC ticket for the ride in. The FEX airport express or the S-Bahn gets you into the centre in about 35–45 minutes. Inside the city an AB ticket covers everything in this guide.

Tuesday 17 NovemberLand, then Sophie Calle, then The Wedding Present

Drop the bags at the Maritim and walk about 20 minutes north-west to the Hamburger Bahnhof, a former railway station turned contemporary-art museum that turns 30 this year. Its new show is Sophie Calle's Is It Better?: photographs, text and found objects, several of them made in Berlin in the 1990s. It is an easy, quiet first afternoon after a flight.

Dinner under the S-Bahn arches at Brauhaus Lemke by Hackescher Markt: house beer and plain, heavy food. Then the U8 south to Neukölln, where The Wedding Present play Seamonsters for its 35th anniversary at Hole44.

Or, if the ML itch wins: the BLISS AI speaker series has Jonas Geiping (Max Planck Institute) at TU Berlin's maths building at 19:00 the same evening. It overlaps with the gig, so it's one or the other.

Wednesday 18 NovemberThe big rock night

Wednesday has more rock on than any other night of the week, so pick one. The default is The Pretty Reckless at Huxleys Neue Welt on Hasenheide, a mid-sized hall where you can still see the stage. If arena scale is the point, Muse play Uber Arena the same night on the Wow! Signal tour, and Papa Roach are at the Velodrom.

Before the show, go to Mehringdamm for Curry 36: currywurst standing at the counter, done in fifteen minutes. Afterwards, Yorckschlösschen is an old Kreuzberg jazz-and-blues pub that stays open late.

Thursday 19 NovemberStreet food, folk metal, craft beer

Leave the conference a little early. Thursday evening is Street Food Thursday at Markthalle Neun, a 19th-century market hall in Kreuzberg. Eat from three stalls, not one.

Then it's about 25 minutes on foot to Huxleys again for Eluveitie: Swiss folk metal with hurdy-gurdy and bagpipes over heavy guitars. Finish at Hopfenreich, one of the city's best craft-beer bars.

The quieter alternative is near the hotel. The rejazz-festival opens that night at Kunstfabrik Schlot, a jazz cellar on Invalidenstraße, with Israeli harmonica player Ariel Bart's trio.

Friday 20 NovemberCoffee and out

One good coffee at The Barn on Auguststraße, a short walk from the hotel, then the train to BER. Allow an hour door to gate, plus the airport's own queues.

Happening this weekWorth catching while you're there

Exhibitions, gigs and one talk that fall inside your four days. Most Christmas markets open from 23 November, after you've left, but six are already running. They're on the Explore map below.
